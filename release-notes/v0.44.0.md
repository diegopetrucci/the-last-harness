## Better terminal integration

- TLH now reports whether it's working, waiting on you, done, idle, or errored to terminals that support program status, so their tab and pane indicators stay accurate.

## Smarter defaults

- The context cap is raised from 200k to 300k tokens, and the "DUMB ZONE" footer warning is gone.
- The `developer` subagent now uses Claude Sonnet 5.5 on Anthropic. The lighter helper subagents use Claude Haiku 5.5.
- Fresh installs use Pi's terminal-adaptive `system` theme.
- Web search is leaner: it now offers just its Exa-backed search and fetch tools.

## Fixes

- Subagent progress is easier to read: the detail lines are aligned, there's one hint per job, and thinking phrases no longer flicker.
- The usage footer stays up to date after model switches and turns.
- `/annotate-git-diff` loads reliably and uses the review colour palette throughout.
