## Updated runtime

- TLH now runs on Pi 1.3.0.

## Smoother long sessions

- Auto-compaction now leaves more headroom, so compaction summaries are less likely to hit the token limit on reasoning models.
- The main session's context cap is now 350k tokens.

## Tidier model picker

- More outdated Anthropic and OpenAI Codex models are hidden from the model picker by default.

## Faster and more reliable

- Launching and listing models are faster.
- On a fresh install, bundled extensions are installed together again instead of one at a time on first launch.
- `tlh update` keeps your managed `gn` and `tk` tools up to date and leaves your own copies alone.
- The footer cost, session limit, and `/tokens` now include cache-warming, tool-result, and compaction spend.

## Fixes

- `tlh update` and `tlh doctor --repair` keep your package filters and pinned or local sources.
- Background subagents pause, resume, and stop more reliably.
- Header lines no longer run past the edge of your terminal.
